Cookie Policy

Last updated: 21 May 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Canary.Hikes uses cookies and similar technologies on this website.

1. What cookies and local storage are

Cookies are small files stored on your device by a website. Local storage is a similar browser technology used to remember settings. They can help a site work, remember choices or measure how people use pages.

2. Essential storage

Essential storage is used to make the site work and remember your choices, such as language preference and cookie consent. These items do not require optional consent because they are needed to provide the service you request.

3. Analytics cookies

If you accept analytics cookies, we use Google Analytics to understand page views, popular content and general website performance. This helps us improve the guides and the website.

Analytics is off by default for users until consent is given through the cookie banner. If you reject analytics cookies, Google Analytics remains disabled for analytics storage.

4. Marketing cookies

We do not currently set advertising or personalised marketing cookies through this website. If this changes, the cookie banner and this policy should be updated before those cookies are used.

5. Cookies and tools we use

Tool Purpose Type
Canary.Hikes local storage Remember language and cookie choices. Essential
Google Analytics Measure website usage and performance after consent. Analytics

6. How to change your choice

You can change your cookie choice at any time by opening cookie settings below. You can also clear cookies and local storage in your browser settings.

7. More information

For information about how we process personal data, read our Privacy Policy.
